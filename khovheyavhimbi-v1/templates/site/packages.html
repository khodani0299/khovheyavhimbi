The place

A stay among trees, water and open views.

Tents sit among old trees on paved platforms. Spend time by the swimming pool or braai area, with a toilet and shower with hot water, mountain and valley views, and a secure fenced facility with an electric gate. String lights bring a soft glow to the evenings.

FAQ

A few useful details.

What is included in a combo?

Combo 3 includes a Mid Range Tent, Camping Bed, Access to Braai Area, Two Bags of Wood, Toilet and Shower with Hot Water, Hiking, a secure facility with a fence and Electric Gate, and Pool. Combo 5 includes the same items with a VIP Tent. Luxury Combo inclusions are to be confirmed.

Can I bring my own tent?

Yes. Camp with your own tent for R400.00.

Are hikes guided?

Please contact us for details.

Do hiking add-ons need a group?

The R250.00 and R350.00 hiking options are noted for groups of 5 or more. Please contact us about group requirements for the R150.00 option.

Do picnics include the pool?

Yes. Swimming pool access is included with Couple, Family and Group picnics.
