About Khovhe Yavhimbi

A quiet corner of the outdoors, made for slowing down.

Khovhe Yavhimbi is a place to breathe out. Wake up in a comfortable tent among old trees, spend an afternoon on a picnic blanket in the open air, or lace up your boots and follow the trails into the hills.

Whether you come for an adventure, a weekend away or simply some peace and quiet, everything here is built around the landscape, so you can switch off and enjoy it.

  • Pool on site
  • Signed hiking trails
